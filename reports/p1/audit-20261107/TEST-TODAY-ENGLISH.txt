PREPARING A NEW CLEAN ROM TEST
- Confirm SM-G780G or SM-G781B Snapdragon and the exact OrangeFox build.
  The latest submitted recovery log identifies R11.3, not R12.0_1.
- Keep backups and a working restoration ROM OUTSIDE internal storage.
  Format Data erases internal storage, including /sdcard/Fox/BACKUPS.
- Use at least 50% battery and reliable storage. The ROM exceeds FAT32's
  per-file limit; use supported internal/exFAT/USB storage.
- Follow the P1r2 ROM instructions, verify the complete ROM ZIP SHA256, reboot
  TO RECOVERY once after successful flash to refresh logical mappings, and
  perform the documented clean-data preparation only with external backups.
  Cache/Dalvik wipe is different from Format Data.
- If flashing fails, save recovery.log and stop; do not boot a partial flash.
- After a failed boot, use CAPTURE above before doing anything else.
